MEMORANDUM — from the family solicitor's private file
Re: the late codicils to the Meridian will

For my own record, the codicils stand as follows:

  · CODICIL OF THE 6th. Executed in my presence and witnessed by two of the
    household. By it the late master names his nephew, Mr. Iveson, as
    principal legatee, over and above his earlier provision. This was the
    master's settled and sanctioned wish; the elevation is proper and stands.

  · Accordingly, should the nephew's people put forward a claim to the principal
    share, that claim is NOT out of order. The unusually high standing is
    explained by this codicil — it is sanctioned, not suspect.

Nothing in these codicils touches the annuities and lesser provisions, which
are governed by the estate ledger.

                                              — the family solicitor
