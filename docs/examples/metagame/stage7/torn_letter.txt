[ a torn half-sheet, the paper singed at one corner ]
[ postmark, struck clear across the fold:  HARWICK  ·  the 14th ]

                                                     Harwick, the 14th

My dear Cousin,

Forgive the hurried hand. I am still kept at Harwick — the packet did not
sail, the master pleading weather, and I have not stirred from this town since
I came to it. I write from the same lodging by the harbour I took on my
arrival, and shall be here yet some days more.

Say nothing of the codicil until I am come. Everything depends upon our being
of one story. Burn this when you have read it.

                                                     — I. M.

[ the remainder of the sheet is torn away ]
