SWORN STATEMENT — on behalf of Miss Isolde Marchmain
Taken at the estate office, in the matter of the Meridian inheritance

I, the undersigned, being lady's maid to Miss Marchmain these six years, do
solemnly declare as follows.

On the morning of the 12th my mistress took the early coach out of Harwick,
bound for the packet. She engaged her passage the week before and I saw the
ticket in her own hand. She sailed that same day and was at sea, out of all
communication, on the night the late codicil was signed at Meridian House.

She could not have been present at the House on the 12th, nor for some days
after, being upon the water. Whatever was done to the will that night, it was
not done by her hand.

This I declare to be true.

                                              — E. Prewitt, lady's maid
