Read EPUB ebooks in your browser

Open an EPUB package as a local, navigable ebook with its chapters and metadata available in a focused browser reader.

Open File ViewerChoose an EPUB file after the viewer opens.

What the EPUB reader supports

  • Book metadata, cover, and table-of-contents inspection.
  • Chapter navigation and readable reflowed content.
  • Embedded images and common ebook styling.
  • A local reading view without an account.

Privacy and limitations

The EPUB archive is unpacked and rendered locally in the default flow. DRM-protected books cannot be unlocked, and publisher-specific scripting or complex fixed layouts may not match a dedicated ebook application.

For fixed-layout documents, use the PDF viewer.
