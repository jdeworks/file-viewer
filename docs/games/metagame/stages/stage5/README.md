# Stage 5 — Signal Racer

**Genre:** lane-runner / rhythm racer. **Theme:** the road is a waveform drawn flat —
race a signal corridor through static, jammers, and rival racers.

## How to play

- Race through **nine rounds** of a scrolling channel. Dodge static `░`, off-beat
  pulses `▒`, and dense blocks `▓`; ride shield lanes `~`; grab boost gates `>>`,
  repairs `+`, and packet caches `$`.
- Watch your **integrity (hull)** — bumping rivals `o` or hitting hazards costs hull.
- A **par ghost** `P` and your **prior-best ghost** `G` set the clock: on later rounds,
  surviving isn't enough — you must beat the par time.
- Spend earned **packets** in the **shop** on upgrades between runs.

## Controls

- **Steer:** `←` / `→` to change lane.
- **At forks:** `↑` commits the **HI** route (gates, more reward), `↓` the **LO** route
  (safer survival), then the channel merges.

## The boss — The Jammer

The Jammer's suppression wave wins **before the race even starts** — but the wave has
a rhythm, and a rhythm can be answered.

> **Un-cheat hint:** the counter-signal isn't in this game — it's in a real audio file,
> `transmission_hum.mp3`. Open it in the media viewer and **play it continuously for one
> full 14-second loop** to calibrate your counter-wave. Then race The Jammer.
