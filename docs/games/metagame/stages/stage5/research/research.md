# Protocol Codex research baseline

The retained deck-builder findings are small readable card text, strong archetype identities,
predictable intents, constrained energy, route tradeoffs, run-long relic synergies, and checkpointed
combat state.

The shipped finale preserves the player's actual deck and overlays a changing sequencing predicate.
Signals land only after the demanded SYN/ACK handshake; ordinary card effects and the normal combat
engine remain active. Base phase HP is 60/80/60 before ascension modifiers. Optional keys lead to the
Kernel of Refusal as a bonus multi-phase climax.

Future work should keep the full finale legible and solvable from its combat presentation.
