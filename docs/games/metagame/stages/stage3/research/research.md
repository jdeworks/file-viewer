# Memory Grid research baseline

The retained nonogram findings are reliable clue logic, low-friction marking controls, deterministic
boards, clear error feedback, and difficulty that adds one concept at a time.

The shipped stage layers larger grids, aliasing, volatile cells, locks, two colours, decay pressure,
economy upgrades, and boon drafts across a corruption ladder. Reaching corruption 8 is the explicit
proof that the player completed the body and directly exposes the Memory Leak.

Future work should keep the gate observable and derived only from solved in-stage snapshots.
