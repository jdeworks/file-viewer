# Fractal Bastion research baseline

The retained tower-defense findings are readable lanes, pre-wave information, distinct tower roles,
targeting control, upgrade forks, refunds when geometry changes, and campaign-scale progression.

The shipped stage layers those systems over five maps and an Armory. The Infinite Loop converts the
same placement/range knowledge into a compact coverage puzzle: three marked points, 100 damage per
covered point, 300 HP total.

Future work should keep every boss coordinate visible and use the normal tower rules to resolve it.
