# Glyph Dungeon research baseline

The retained roguelike findings are readable ASCII silhouettes, short tactical turns, meaningful
terrain, constrained healing, persistent meta-progression, and escalating biome rules.

The shipped stage applies those findings across nine floors and three acts. Element interactions,
runes, darkness, traps, sub-bosses, Heat, banking, and the shop create varied runs. The final boss is
a persistent attrition challenge: every legal strike advances the fight and every strike has an HP
cost.

Future work should deepen dungeon decisions without adding a progression dependency outside the
stage overlay.
