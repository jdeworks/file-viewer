# Stage 2 — Glyph Dungeon

**Genre:** turn-based ASCII roguelike. **Theme:** descend nine floors of a glyph
dungeon across three acts — the Warrens, the Cisterns & Emberworks, and the Overflow.

## How to play

- Descend floor by floor. Reach the `>` **stairs** to go deeper; deeper means harder
  foes and better loot.
- **Walk into a foe to attack** (your ATK vs its HP) — it hits back, so watch your HP.
- Pick up `/` weapons (raise ATK) and `%` glyph shards. Kills drop glyphs and XP.
- Dying or **retreating banks** the run's glyphs permanently; the **shop** spends
  banked glyphs on upgrades that apply next run. Optional **Heat** modifiers raise
  risk for a bigger glyph multiplier.

## Controls

- **Move:** arrow keys, **WASD**, or the on-screen d-pad — one tile per press.
- **Attack:** move into an adjacent foe.
- **Runes:** pick up `♦` runes, then press **1–5** (or the rune buttons):
  **1** blink, **2** firebolt, **3** freeze, **4** torch, **5** acid flask.
- Combine systems — firebolt a spore field, freeze then shatter, slide a foe into a
  chasm on ice.

## Bosses

Each act ends in a pink **Ω guardian** on the stairs; beat it to pass. The floor-9
boss is **THE AMBIGUOUS EXPRESSION**, and it starts **locked**.

> **Un-cheat hint:** the boss won't even open until you've *read the room's note*.
> There's a `cipher.txt` lying in the viewer — open it and **search it** for the word
> it's protecting. Find the passage, and the boss gate lifts.
