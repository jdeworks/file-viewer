# Bit Foundry research baseline

The retained design findings are the value of a legible incremental economy, milestone-based
disclosure, short feedback loops, meaningful automation, and prestige mechanics that change play
rather than only multiplying output.

The shipped stage applies those findings through generator tiers, managers, cores, Defrag Echoes,
resonance ratios, Flux bursts, entropy pressure, achievements, and the Defragmenter finale. The boss
uses one canonical fair simulation. Reachability depends on completing the economy; victory depends
on sustained rhythm and warm-burst timing.

Future tuning should preserve deterministic simulation, honest boss feedback, and full in-overlay
completion.
