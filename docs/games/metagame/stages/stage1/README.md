# Stage 1 — Bit Foundry

**Genre:** idle / incremental clicker. **Theme:** you are forging raw *bits* in a
machine foundry, one tap at a time, until the production line runs itself.

## How to play

- **Tap / click the foundry** (the big press in the middle) to earn bits by hand.
- Once you pass ~150 lifetime bits the **tabs unlock** — switch between **Bits**
  (shop) and **Achievements**.
- In the **Bits** shop, buy generator tiers — multipliers, boxes, boosts, clusters,
  arrays, neural cores, quantum cores. Each tier produces bits automatically.
- **Hire managers** to automate a tier so it keeps buying/earning without you.
- Buy buttons never grey out mid-press — a tier you can't afford just shows locked.

## Controls

- **Click / tap** the press to earn; **click** shop and buy buttons.
- **Click the tabs** to move between Bits and Achievements.
- Everything is pointer-driven — no keyboard needed.

## Economy & replay

- A **score chip** appears at 400 lifetime bits; help unlocks deeper in.
- **Prestige** resets your bits for permanent **cores**, and each prestige unlocks
  one new mechanic (echoes, resonance, flux, entropy…) for a stronger restart.

## The boss — The Defragmenter

Reach the boss only after you own at least one of every generator tier, then
**Confront**. It's a tap-duel… that you **cannot win by tapping**. The Defragmenter
cheats, and it keeps cheating no matter how fast you click.

> **Un-cheat hint:** the boss isn't beating you — a *rule written in a file* is. The
> Defragmenter keeps muttering about a config it would *never* want you to edit. This
> game window won't help you. Find that file in the viewer, open it for real, and
> change the line it's hiding. Then come back and fight.
