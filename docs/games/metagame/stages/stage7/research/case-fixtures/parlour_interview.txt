PARLOUR INTERVIEW — the housekeeper's questioning of the claimants
(taken down in the parlour, in the presence of the solicitor)

HOUSEKEEPER. You say you knew the house well. Then which window of the west
wing looks upon the walled garden?

MR. HALLORAN.  The second from the corner — the one with the cracked pane the
mistress would never have mended. (Correct, and plainly remembered.)

MRS. TREVISICK. The second from the corner. I sat under it many an evening.
(Correct.)

MR. ONSLOW.    The second, I believe. (Correct, though hesitant.)

MR. PEVERELL.  (a pause) The — the third? The one nearest the door.
(Wrong. He faltered, and could not say which garden.)

NOTE: the true members of the household answered plainly and at once. The one
who faltered had learned his part, not lived it.
