THE MERIDIAN ESTATE — RULES OF THE INHERITANCE
(as settled by the late master and recognised by the family solicitor)

1. The estate passes to the true heir of the blood, or to such person as the
   will and its codicils name.

2. Kinship BY MARRIAGE is a recognised connection. A great-aunt by marriage, a
   companion long in the household, and the like may put forward a claim; such a
   claim is not irregular merely for being by marriage rather than by blood.

3. No person may hold more than ONE standing engagement to the estate at a time
   (steward, agent, companion, clerk). An engagement, once given up or pensioned
   off, is CLOSED and confers no continuing right.

4. A claimant's standing is proved by the will, the codicils, and the household
   records — not by assertion. Where a record and a claim disagree, the record
   governs.
