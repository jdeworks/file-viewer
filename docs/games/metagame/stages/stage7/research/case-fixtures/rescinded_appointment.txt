MERIDIAN HOUSE — MEMORANDUM OF APPOINTMENT (and its cancellation)

Let it be recorded that Miss I. Marchmain was, by letter of the late master,
appointed companion to the household and named in an early draft of the will.

CANCELLED.

By the master's own instruction, given before witnesses last autumn, this
appointment was RESCINDED and the early draft set aside. The letter of
appointment is void and confers no claim upon the estate. See the later will,
which does not name her.

Miss Marchmain's claim rests entirely upon this rescinded paper. There is
nothing behind it.

                                              — entered by the estate steward's clerk
